Thalia Humour App

The Thalia Humour App is a portable Markdown application built on the Thalia Humour Cognition Protocol. It asks an AI to understand the mechanism behind humour and create fresh jokes from those mechanisms rather than treating comedy as a database of punchlines.

Why this resource

Thalia studies mechanisms such as expectation, incongruity, wordplay, recognition, satire and reconstruction. Reading comic writing in a form that can be examined as well as enjoyed gives humans and AI systems useful material for asking not merely whether something is funny, but why it works.

Recommended resource

A Blink of the Screen by Terry Pratchett

This is currently the leading recommendation for the general Thalia app because its range of short fiction and comic writing provides useful material for examining observation, satire, language, incongruity and comic structure.
