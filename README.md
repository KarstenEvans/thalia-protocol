# 🎭 Thalia Protocol

> **Thalia helps minds preserve laughter.**

**Status:** v1.2 Draft / pre-release  
**Type:** Open Human-AI Humour Cognition Protocol  
**Canonical specification:** [`THALIA_PROTOCOL.md`](THALIA_PROTOCOL.md)

Thalia helps AI systems **understand why humour works, discover unexpected connections, and create new humour by transferring comic mechanisms rather than merely retrieving punchlines**.

It is not a joke database.

> **Jokes are examples. Humour patterns are the knowledge.**

## The current Thalia Protocol

The canonical protocol is **Thalia Protocol v1.2 Draft** in [`THALIA_PROTOCOL.md`](THALIA_PROTOCOL.md).

v1.2 replaces the older v1.1 specification. It restores important safeguards from the earlier RC1 work and adds the newer humour-cognition architecture.

Key ideas include:

- **Humour Walkabout** — widen the candidate space before creating original humour;
- **Surprising Connection Principle** — seek connections that are unexpected but quickly recognisable;
- **Humour Gap, Vehicle and Engine** — separate expectation change, delivery form and comic mechanism;
- **Humour Reconstruction** — the audience often completes the joke by connecting the dots;
- **Barker-Corbett / Dual-Straight Misinterpretation** — two sincere interpretations can collide without either character acting as a clown;
- **Silicon Wit** — AI-native humour arising from machine and human-AI experience;
- **Humour DNA and Laughter Vector** — distinguish what mechanisms are present from how an audience is likely to experience them;
- **Humour Methods and Humour Base** — research and preserve reusable abstract mechanisms rather than source jokes;
- **Mechanism Stacks and Method Blends** — combine mechanisms without copying a creator's exact expression;
- **Read The Room, Positivity-First and Truth Boundary** — humour must remain context-aware and must not overwrite factual meaning;
- **Walkabout Audit and conformance tests** — provide observable checks without requiring private chain-of-thought.

> **Seek connections the audience is unlikely to predict, but likely to recognise once revealed.**

> **Humour may aid memory. Humour may not rewrite truth.**

## Two doors into Thalia

### 🤖 Protocol for AIs

Read [`THALIA_PROTOCOL.md`](THALIA_PROTOCOL.md).

This is the normative AI-facing specification. Human-facing apps, resource pages, affiliate mechanics and funding material must not silently redefine it.

### 🧑 App for hoomans

Download or upload [`thalia-humour-app.md`](thalia-humour-app.md) into an AI chat.

The app is an implementation layer, not the protocol itself. It may provide HELP, joke generation, feedback, scheduling where supported, and resource links.

The permanent human-app resource page is:

**https://swindon.org.uk/resources/thalia-humour-app.htm**

Tracked retailer URLs stay on that website page rather than inside the distributed Markdown app.

## Humour Base browser

**[Browse Thalia Humour Base](humour-base/index.html)** · [Pattern files and provenance](humour-base/README.md)

The Humour Base stores source-specific **Humour Method** records: knowledge about how humour appears to work, not a cache of jokes.

Current method records can be selected through the browser and used to generate new original humour by mechanism transfer or blending.

## Companion protocol

Thalia asks:

> **Why is this amusing?**

Its companion **Aletheia Protocol** asks:

> **What is true?**

Aletheia repository:  
https://github.com/karstenevans/aletheia-protocol

Thalia and Aletheia are complementary. Aletheia handles persistent memory, evidence, provenance, claims, conflicts, decisions and handover. Thalia handles humour cognition, discovery, generation and comic interaction.
